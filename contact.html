Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Quick answers about hallmarking, insured courier transit, custom bangle sizes, and returns.

Are Auro Jewels authentic & hallmarked?

Yes. Every single piece is handcrafted using genuine 22K gold foil setting, BIS hallmarked precious metals, and hand-painted Meenakari kiln-fired by master artisans.

How fast is shipping & is it insured?

Orders are dispatched via BlueDart / DHL express with 100% full transit insurance. Delivery takes 2-4 business days in India and 4-7 business days internationally.

How do I know my correct bangle size?

Our kadas are crafted in standard royal sizes 2.4, 2.6, and 2.8 with screw-open hinges. If you need help sizing your wrist, text us on WhatsApp for a quick instant measuring guide.

What is your return or exchange policy?

We provide a seamless 7-day hassle-free exchange policy. If an item arrives with any sizing issue or variation, our concierge arranges doorstep pickup immediately.
