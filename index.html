Artisanal Pedigree

The Secret Behind Authentic Meenakari

Originating from the royal courts of Rajasthan, Meenakari is the intricate art of engraving delicate metal crevices and filling them with vibrant mineral enamels heated at blistering temperatures over kiln fire.

01

Chitrai (Hand Engraving)

Master chiteras hand-chisel floral jaals and peacock plumes into high-grade alloy.

02

Rangbhari (Mineral Enamelling)

Ground lapis, ruby powder, and turquoise glass pastes are painted with squirrel-hair brushes.

03

Jadau & Kundan Setting

Pure 24K gold leaves (Daak) frame each uncut polki glass stone to catch ambient candlelight.
