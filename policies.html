Insured Express Shipping Policy

Every piece of jewellery leaving our Jaipur studio is packed in velvet-lined luxury keepsake boxes, secured with tamper-evident seals, and tracked with real-time GPS courier logistics.

Complimentary Domestic Shipping: All domestic orders above ₹3,999 qualify for free air-express delivery via BlueDart or Delhivery.

7-Day Hassle-Free Exchange Policy

We want you to treasure every heirloom jewellery piece you acquire. If you have an unexpected sizing mismatch on a kada or wish to exchange a design, we make it effortless.

Easy Doorstep Pickup: Simply text our concierge on WhatsApp (+91 98200 12345) within 7 days of delivery. We arrange a reverse pickup directly from your doorstep with zero return hassle.

Items must be unworn, in original velvet packaging with security tag intact. Once inspected, replacement or store credit voucher is issued within 24 hours.

BIS Hallmarking & Authenticity Guarantee

Auro & Jewellary strictly complies with Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) hallmarking guidelines. Every piece carries our official laser-engraved hallmark guaranteeing metal purity and genuine gemstone certification.

Our traditional Meenakari enamelling is kiln-fired with natural minerals, and uncut Jadau Polki stones are mounted using authentic 22K gold foil setting passed down through royal craft guilds.

Lifetime Re-Polishing & Craft Care

Heirloom jewellery is meant to be passed down through generations. Auro & Jewellary provides lifetime re-polishing and stone re-tightening support for all our bespoke clients at any of our flagship boutiques in Jaipur and Mumbai.

256-Bit SSL Encrypted Luxury Checkout

Your payment credentials and personal information are guarded with bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption. We support all major UPI gateways, Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and PayPal with zero card storage.
