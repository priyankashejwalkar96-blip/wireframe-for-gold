Privacy Policy

Your privacy and personal discretion are paramount at Auro & Jewellary. Learn how we collect, safeguard, and respect your confidential data.

Last Updated: November 2026 • 256-Bit SSL Secured

1. Overview & Commitment

Auro & Jewellary ("we", "our", "us") values the trust you place in us when sharing your personal information. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, transmission, and secure preservation across our online store, digital lookbooks, and private concierge consultations.

2. Information We Collect

To provide high-end, bespoke jewellery shopping experiences and ensure insured delivery, we collect:

  • Contact Details: Full name, telephone/WhatsApp contact, billing and insured shipping addresses.
  • Transaction Data: Order records, payment confirmation tokens (we do NOT store credit/debit card numbers; all transactions process through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways).
  • Device & Usage Information: IP address, device telemetry, browser type, and navigation paths to optimize site performance.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data exclusively for legitimate luxury commerce purposes:

  • Dispatching insured domestic and international air courier shipments with tamper-proof tracking.
  • Communicating real-time order milestones and scheduling boutique consultations.
  • Fulfilling BIS Hallmarking records and lifetime warranty certificates under Indian Bureau standards.
  • Preventing fraudulent transactions and unauthorized gateway attempts.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We strictly never rent, monetize, or sell customer records. Data is solely disclosed to vetted logistics partners (BlueDart, Sequel Logistics, DHL Express) for order delivery, and statutory authorities where required by prevailing Indian law.

5. Security & Encryption

Our platform enforces end-to-end 256-bit SSL encryption, automated DDoS shields, and strict access controls. Your communications with our concierge via WhatsApp and telephone remain confidential.

6. Contact Our Grievance Officer

For inquiries regarding your personal information or to request record deletion, please contact our Data Governance Officer at privacy@aurojewels.com or call our Concierge at +91 98200 12345.
