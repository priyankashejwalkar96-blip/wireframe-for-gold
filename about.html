Chapter I • Johari Bazaar Roots

From the Royal Havelis to Global Runways

In the 16th century, imperial patronage in the pink corridors of Jaipur nurtured the rarest enamelling art on earth: Meenakari. Our great-grandfather Shri Radheshyamji was awarded court commendation for creating royal ceremonial kadas, sirpech, and bridal ornaments for Rajasthani dynasties.

Today, Auro & Jewellary brings these heirloom-grade techniques to the modern bride worldwide, without the prohibitive real-gold surcharge, using hallmarked allergy-free alloys and authentic hand-cut Polki stones.

Chapter II • The Craft Philosophy

Luxury That Honors Both Heritage & Skin

Traditional bridal jewelry often forces a difficult choice between exorbitant pure gold costs or cheap synthetic metals that irritate sensitive skin. We rewrote this standard.

Every Auro creation is sculpted in pure brass or certified German silver, encased in triple-layer 22K micro-micron gold plating, and finished with baked mineral glass enameling that never fades or peels under tropical humidity.

Each piece is stamped with our emblem of authenticity and passes 4 rigorous quality controls before it is sealed in velvet-lined vault packaging.

Artisanal Pedigree

The Anatomy of Authentic Meenakari

A single bridal bangle takes up to 72 hours of meticulous manual handwork across four specialized guild craftsmen.

01

Ghaat (Moulding)

The core structural form is hand-cast with precision screw joints, custom calibrated for ergonomic balance.

02

Chitrai (Engraving)

Master engravers chisel delicate peacocks, lotuses, and floral creepers into the metal surface using steel burins.

03

Rangbhari (Kiln)

Crushed mineral glass pigments are infused into the grooves and baked in traditional kilns at 850°C for indelible color.

04

Ghothai (Polishing)

Natural agate stones burnish the gold surface to a mirror-like royal finish without abrasive chemical degradation.
