The Birthplace of Royalty

From the Havelis of Johari Bazaar

In the 16th century, Raja Man Singh I brought master enamellers from Lahore to the pink walls of Jaipur. Our great-grandfather Shri Radheshyamji was awarded imperial patronage to create royal ceremonial kadas and turban sirpech for the royal house.

Today, Auro & Jewellary brings these museum-grade techniques to the modern bride worldwide, without the prohibitive real-gold surcharge, using hallmarked allergy-free alloys.
