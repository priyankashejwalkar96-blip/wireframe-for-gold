Authenticity & Purity

Hallmarking & Purity Disclosure

Transparency regarding metallurgical composition, certified German silver standards, 22K micro-micron gold plating, and BIS hallmarking compliance.

Official Purity Charter • BIS & Anti-Allergic Certified

1. The Auro Hallmark of Authenticity

At Auro & Jewellary, every ornament is stamped with our signature atelier hallmark. We ensure complete transparency regarding the materials, stone settings, and plating techniques employed across our bridal and everyday luxury collections.

Lead & Nickel Free

100% hypoallergenic, compliant with international REACH dermatological safety tests.

22K Micro-Micron

Triple electroplated gold layers with baked protective lacquer against tropical tarnish.

Authentic Jadau Daak

Hand-cut Polki stones mounted in genuine 24K pure gold foil reflex cups.

2. Demifine & German Silver Specifications

Our oxidized and silver collections utilize certified German Silver (a premium non-corrosive copper-nickel-zinc jewelry alloy) and 92.5 Sterling Silver where specified. Every piece undergoes ultrasonic cleansing and passivation to prevent greening or skin discoloration.

3. Traditional Mineral Meenakari Disclosure

Our vitreous enamel (Meenakari) is crafted from crushed natural silica, cobalt, copper, and ruby oxides fired in traditional wood and electric kilns at temperatures exceeding 850°C. This vitreous glass fusion will never peel, bubble, or fade with age.

4. Authenticity Certificate

Every bespoke bridal set and luxury curation is shipped with a physical Authenticity Card and serial certificate stating batch inspection date, karigar guild signature, and lifetime re-polishing privilege.
